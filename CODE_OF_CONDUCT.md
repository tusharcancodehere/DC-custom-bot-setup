# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

DC Custom Bot is an open-source project intended to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone who wants to use, contribute to, or discuss the project.

We expect all contributors and community members to participate professionally and respectfully.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

* Be respectful and considerate of others.
* Communicate constructively, even when opinions differ.
* Give useful and respectful feedback.
* Focus criticism on ideas, code, or contributions rather than people.
* Respect different levels of experience.
* Keep discussions relevant to the project.
* Follow the project's contribution and security guidelines.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or hateful conduct.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Trolling or deliberate disruption.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content.
* Publishing private or sensitive information about others.
* Deliberately exposing credentials, tokens, or other secrets.
* Malicious behavior toward contributors, users, or project infrastructure.

---

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreement is normal and encouraged when it is constructive.

When discussing architecture, code, or design:

* Explain your reasoning.
* Focus on technical merits.
* Provide evidence or examples when useful.
* Be open to alternative approaches.
* Keep disagreements professional.

A rejected proposal is not a personal rejection of its author.

---

## Project Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for keeping the community productive and respectful.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or take other appropriate action when community guidelines are violated.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from project spaces.

---

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers through the project's designated communication channel.

For security vulnerabilities, **do not use public issues**. Follow the reporting process described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Please provide enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private information.

---

## Enforcement

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Actions taken may depend on the severity and circumstances of the incident.

The project maintains the right to remove participation privileges when necessary to protect the community.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions related to DC Custom Bot, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and official community spaces where this code is adopted.

---

## Acknowledgment

By participating in the DC Custom Bot community, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful and constructive environment.
